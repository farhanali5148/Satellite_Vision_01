How scoring works
  • Each indicator is compared with that astronaut's pre-flight baseline, in the direction that matters for that indicator.
  • Within the watch threshold scores stay at 85–100. Past it the score falls through Watch (55–85) into Act (below 55).
  • A domain score blends the average with the worst indicator, so one bad signal is not hidden by good ones.
Prototype for decision support on simulated data. It is not a medical device. Thresholds are illustrative and would be set with flight surgeons for a real mission.
